Hello, hello! I'm Henry Daggett.

I believe my experience building and evolving large-scale design systems, bridging design and engineering and developing new tooling could be of real value to Barclaycard Payments as you shape your Design System.

In my seven years leading design systems I can tell you that I've faced just about every single problem that a system or product team could possibly have. My experience designing financial products has also given me a strong foundation for dealing with complexity and building systems that can accommodate it.

Design/Build

I've worked across a wide range of diverse products, including large trading tools, transactions, prime services, monitoring, development, and marketing sites. I'm comfortable working on high-level flows down to granular interaction design. I've also built and maintained multiple Design System libraries in Figma.

Although design is still my primary focus, I'm happy to dive in and build too. The closer that designers are able to collaborate with engineers, the better. In the last few years I've been becoming more technical, to the extent that I'm now contributing production code to products and design systems.

This year AI has been a huge focus in my work (of course), from utilising it to build tools and prototype to designing chat and generative experiences for advanced users.
